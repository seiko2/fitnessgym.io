06 / GOOD TO KNOW

LET’S CLEAR
THINGS UP.

Still have a question?
We’re a message away ↗

How do I become a member?+

Membership is open from age 12. Choose a plan, complete the registration details, and save your card. Members aged 12–17 must be represented by a parent or guardian, who supplies their contact details and confirms consent. Visit the gym to make payment and confirm arrangements with staff.

Is a personal trainer included?+

Personal training is optional and charged separately. Trainer add-ons are UGX 15,000 for daily, UGX 50,000 for monthly, UGX 150,000 for quarterly, and UGX 600,000 for annual membership. Confirm session arrangements with the gym.

What are your opening hours?+

Our opening hours are 8:00 a.m. to midnight (00:00), Kampala time. Contact us to confirm holiday opening and trainer availability.

Can I print my membership card?+

Yes. Your card is generated after registration. Choose “Print / save PDF” and use your browser’s print options. You can return to your member area on the same device to print it again.

How do I renew my membership?+

Contact the gym or speak with reception to renew. Once staff record your renewal and payment, your member area will show the updated expiry date.

How is my information used?+

Your registration details are stored securely for membership administration. Your height and weight help staff keep an accurate fitness record. They are visible only in your private member record and to authorised gym staff. For members aged 12–17, we also store the representing parent or guardian’s details. Your public card verification shows only your name, plan, membership status, and expiry date.
